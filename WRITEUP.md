# Red Light: write-up

## Tagline

One click. The whole screen goes red.

## Profile description

Red Light is a small menu bar app for macOS. Click the bulb icon, and each display shifts to red light. Click again, and the normal colours come back. Control-Option-Command-R does the same from any app. A right-click opens three presets (Red, Dim Red and Amber) and three sliders for the red, green and blue channels. You can save a setting as a preset of your own. The app is one Swift file. It stores only your presets, and it uses no network.

## Use cases

- Late work in a dark room: keep the screen on and make it red.
- A shared bedroom: use Dim Red while another person sleeps.
- Stargazing, a cockpit or a boat at night: read the screen in red light.
